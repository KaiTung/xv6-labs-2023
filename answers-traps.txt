2.Where is the call to function f in the assembly code for main? Where is the call to g? (Hint: the compiler may inline functions.)
    1. Where is the call to f?
    In the assembly code for main, there’s no explicit jal or jalr to f. However, you see this instruction:

    26:	45b1                	li	a1,12

    This corresponds to the computation of f(8) + 1.

    f(8) is inlined: The computation g(x) (which is x + 3) and the addition of 1 are performed directly in main. Specifically:
    g(8) computes 8 + 3 = 11.
    Adding 1 to this result gives 12.
    Since the compiler has inlined f and g, there’s no need to explicitly call these functions.

    Similarly, there is no explicit call to g. Its behavior (x + 3) is included directly in the computation within f and further inlined in main

4.What value is in the register ra just after the jalr to printf in main?
    The jalr instruction is at address 0x34. After it executes, the return address will be the address of the next instruction:
